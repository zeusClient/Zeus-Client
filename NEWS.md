# New look for Zeus Crates
The crates screen is redesigned and much lighter to run.

- Sharp new text and layout that look the same at every GUI scale
- A spotlight stage for the crate, with Buy key and Quick open beside Open
- The Chroma knife of each crate gets a big featured tile
- New Chroma textures for the karambit, M9, crystal and phoenix
- Opening a crate draws far less, so the reel and reveal stay smooth
- Number keys 1 to 5 switch tabs, Esc closes a knife you are inspecting

# New: Chroma knives
Six secret knives, one in every crate. About 1 in 400 opens.

- Galaxy karambit, Molten Core M9, Prism Shard, Gilded Phoenix, Stardust butterfly, Spectrum kunai
- Chroma and Olympian blades glow
- Butterfly knives now flip like real ones: blade and handles swing round the pins
- Chroma drops get their own rainbow reveal
- New crate cases: the latches click and the lid swings open
- Knives sit in your hand and dim in the dark

# New: keybinds keyboard
Keybinds are now a full virtual keyboard. Click a key, pick modules from the list, done.

- Several modules on one key toggle together
- Press a key on your real keyboard to jump to it
- Open it with /zeus keybinds

# Zeus Crates
Open crates from the main menu and unbox HD knives.

- A crate takes 3 keys, a key costs 10 tokens
- New DAILY tab: 3 quests a day for tokens
- Daily streak: play 3 minutes a day for bonus tokens
- Divine or better guaranteed every 25 opens
- Got a promo code? Use the Redeem tab
